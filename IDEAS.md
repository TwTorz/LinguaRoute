## Chosen idea
**Name:** LinguaRoute
**One-liner:** Our project helps global customer support teams quickly detect the language of incoming customer messages and route them to the right agent faster.
**MVP (must have):**
- load multilingual text dataset
- filter to English, Spanish, Japanese, and Mandarin
- train a simple language classifier
- predict language from a message
- show the result in a UI

**Nice to have (only if time allows):**
- route to language-specific support queue
- confidence score
- failure examples

AI task: detect the language of a short customer message and route it to the right support queue.
